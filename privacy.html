Patika Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 8, 2026

Patika: Habits & Life Path ("Patika", "we") helps you plan routines, tasks and events and see your progress as a path. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We keep it short because we collect very little.

What we collect

  • Account details: your name and email address, when you sign in with Apple, Google or email. If you use "Hide My Email" with Apple, we only see the relay address.
  • What you add in the app: your routines, tasks, events, check-ins, flags, reminder times and settings. This is stored so your path follows you across devices.
  • Subscription status: whether you have Patika Premium or a free trial. Payments are handled by Apple; we never see your card details.

We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or health data. We do not use advertising, ad tracking or analytics SDKs, and we do not sell your data.

How we use it

  • To run the app: show your path, sync your data and send the reminders you turned on.
  • To unlock Premium when you subscribe and restore it on a new device.
  • To answer you if you contact us.

Services we use

  • Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) to sign you in and store your data. Firebase privacy
  • RevenueCat to manage subscriptions. It receives an anonymous account ID and your purchase history from Apple. RevenueCat privacy
  • Sign in with Apple and Google Sign-In, if you choose them.
  • Google Fonts: the app may download its fonts from Google's servers, which see your IP address like any web request.

Reminders are scheduled on your device. Images you share (like your path card) are created on your device and only go where you send them.

Keeping and deleting your data

We keep your data while you have an account. You can delete your account and all your data anytime in the app: Profile → Settings → Delete account. This removes your data from our database right away. An active subscription is managed by Apple, so please also cancel it in your Apple ID settings.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR), you can ask to see, correct, export or delete your data, or object to how we use it. Write to us and we will help.

Children

Patika is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
