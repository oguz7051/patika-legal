Common questions

How do I add the widget?
Touch and hold your home screen, tap Edit, then Add Widget, and find Patika. The widget is part of Premium.

How do I cancel my subscription?
Open the Settings app on your iPhone, tap your name, then Subscriptions, and choose Patika. You can cancel anytime; Premium stays on until the end of the period you paid for.

I changed phones. Where is my path?
Sign in with the same account you used before (Apple, Google or email) and your path comes back. To get Premium back, tap Restore on the Premium screen.

How do I delete my account?
In the app: Profile, Settings, Delete account. This removes your account and all your data.
