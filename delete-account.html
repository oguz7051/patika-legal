Delete your Patika account

This page explains how to delete your account in Patika: Life Graph & Habits and what happens to your data.

In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Patika and go to Profile.
  2. Tap Settings.
  3. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account and all your data are deleted right away.

Without the app

Email oguz7051@gmail.com from the address you signed up with (or tell us that address, if you used Sign in with Apple or Google), with the subject "Delete my Patika account". We delete the account and its data within 7 days and confirm by email.

What is deleted

  • Your account (name, email address and user ID).
  • Everything you added: routines, tasks, events, check-ins, notes, flags and settings, on your phone and in the cloud backup.
  • Your member record (sign-up date, last visit and subscription status).

What is kept

  • Nothing from Patika itself. Deletion is permanent and can't be undone.
  • Purchase records are kept by Google Play or the App Store, and by our payment provider RevenueCat, as their own rules and tax laws require. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription: cancel it in Google Play or in your iPhone settings.
